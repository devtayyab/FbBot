# Local notes

Claude uses these notes as trusted facts when it writes replies. It gives prices, opening hours,
phone numbers, distances and rules only when they appear here, so put the facts you want in replies here.
Short bullet points work best. Replace the examples and update the notes whenever something changes.

## Getting around
- (example) Ride apps work well from the airport; agree the fare before getting into a street taxi.

## Where to stay
- (example) First-time visitors like the old town: walkable, close to cafes and sights.

## Food
- (example) The night market opens after sunset and is the best place for street food.

## Must-see
- (example) Main square, the old fort, the sunset viewpoint by the river.

## Safety and practical
- (example) Get a local SIM at the airport; bring your passport.

## Things we never say
- (example) No visa advice; point people to the official government website.
